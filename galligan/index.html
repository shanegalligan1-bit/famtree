    How the research is done

    Living people are recorded only from what the family has said and are never searched for. Irish civil birth records are public after 100 years, marriages after 75 and deaths after 50; the 1901, 1911 and 1926 censuses are public in full. Ages in old records drift — a census age can be five or ten years out — so birth years marked "c." are estimates from several sources.

    Bring a tree in, or take ours out

    GEDCOM is the file every family-tree site speaks — Ancestry, MyHeritage, FamilySearch, Gramps. If a relative has built a tree anywhere, export it there and drop it here: people we already have are linked, not duplicated; new people arrive in amber until a record backs them.
